Design system & UI spec · v1.0 · 2026-09-26

ŠvaraPro: švaresniam gyvenimui

This is the full UI system for a cleaning service in Lithuania: tokens, components, behavior, the booking flow, and hi-fi screens. The layout follows your reference. Everything on this page is interactive: scroll inside the frames, drag the before/after slider, change the calculator, and click through the booking steps. A developer should be able to build the site from this page and design/tokens.css.

Q1Where am I?

The active nav item is underlined, pages have breadcrumbs, and every booking step shows “5 / 7” plus the step name.

Q2What can I do here?

Each section has one job and one main action. The price is visible without clicking anything.

Q3What next?

Primary buttons always name the next step (“Tęsti”, “Patvirtinti užsakymą”), and the confirmation page lists what happens after booking.

02 · Visual direction

“A room right after it was cleaned”

The interface itself should feel like a clean flat on a bright October morning. That comes from space instead of decoration, a single deep-green voice against warm off-white, and soft, generous curves. The large curved corner on the hero image and on the green panels is the one signature shape. It echoes a wiped surface, and it is used only on the hero image, the calculator intro panel, and the CTA band.

Hierarchy comes from spacing, borders, and surface contrast first. Shadows stay whisper-light. Lime #B7E36A is a spark and appears in only three places: the “available” dot, checkmarks on dark green, and a faint glow on the calculator panel.

Local details that make it Lithuanian: price ranges written “54–68 €” (Lithuanian convention: euro sign after the number, en dash, no spaces; your reference mixed “€54” and “35 €”, so this unifies them), “m²” as the unit, dates like “spalio 2 d., penktadienis”, the +370 phone prefix, VAT (PVM) noted on prices, weeks starting on Monday (Pr–S), and city names with full diacritics (Klaipėda, Šiauliai, Panevėžys).

FOTO

Photography brief

Use
  • Natural daylight from a window, soft shadows, morning or noon
  • Real Lithuanian or Nordic apartments: oak floors, linen, plants, white walls with warm undertone
  • Specialists in a green uniform, natural poses, working rather than smiling at the camera
  • Detail shots of a hand wiping a counter or a folded cloth on oak
  • A consistent grade: slightly warm, low contrast, no HDR
Avoid
  • Blown-out, fake-white 3D rooms
  • Big staged grins, thumbs up, crossed arms
  • American suburban kitchens and stock “cleaning lady” clichés
  • Cartoons, 3D blobs, bubbles, sparkle stickers
  • Mixing illustration and photography on the same page

Iconography: Lucide, one family

Stroke 1.85px (1.6px at 28px and above), round caps. Sizes 16 / 20 / 24 / 32. On surfaces, icons sit in a soft-green circle (trust, steps) or a rounded 12px square (options). Never mix filled and outline icons, except stars.

03 · Color system

One deep green, warm neutrals, calm feedback colors

Contrast ratios are calculated live below. Text tokens are checked against white and the page background, and surfaces are checked against primary text. --color-text-secondary is darkened from #66716D to #5F6A66 so it passes AA on tinted surfaces too. Form borders use --color-border-input (3.1:1) to meet WCAG 1.4.11.

Usage rules

  • Green is for things you can click or things that are selected. Don’t use it for decorative headings.
  • Use at most one filled green button per viewport section. Everything else is secondary or tertiary.
  • Tinted bands (surface-mint) alternate with background. Never stack two tinted sections.
  • Feedback colors always come with an icon and text. Color is never the only signal.
  • No pure black (#000) and no pure grey. Every neutral is biased slightly toward the green.
04 · Typography

Manrope, 400–700

Manrope has open, geometric forms with a friendly “a” and “g”, full Lithuanian diacritics (ą č ę ė į š ų ū ž), and tabular figures for prices. Headlines use 700 with tight tracking, H3–H4 use 600, body text uses 400/500. 800 is never used. Body text is never smaller than 16px on mobile.

Rules

  • Line length: 45–70 characters. Hero description max 40ch, FAQ answers max 60ch.
  • Headings use text-wrap: balance. Keep the unit with the number using non-breaking spaces: “65 m²”, “Nuo 79 €”.
  • Prices and times use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so the value doesn’t jitter when it updates.
  • Section eyebrows are pill tags (13px/600) with an icon, sentence case. Uppercase is only for captions.
  • Never use bold inside running body text for emphasis. Use primary text color at weight 600 instead.

09 · Forms & feedback

Ask little, explain errors, confirm everything

Structure: Label → Input → Helper → Error. The placeholder is only an example (“Pvz.: 612 34567”) and never replaces the label. Mark optional fields with “(nebūtina)”. Everything unmarked is required.

Validation timing: validate a field on blur only once it has been touched, and validate everything on “Tęsti”. Errors clear live as the user fixes them. On submit, focus moves to the first invalid field.

Layout: one column on mobile, always. On desktop, two columns only for short pairs such as flat number + floor.

Formatting: the phone field has a fixed “+370” prefix and accepts “612 34567”, “861234567”, or “+37061234567”, then normalises. The area field accepts “65,5”.

Loading: loading happens inside the button, never as a full-page spinner. Lists use skeletons, and the price uses an inline 200ms swap.

Error UX: never raw codes

11 · Navigation

Header: transparent, then quietly solid

Desktop header · 78px

  • Left: logo (36 mark + wordmark + “Švaresniam gyvenimui”). Center: 6 items, 14.5/500, 44h targets. Right: language (LT ▾) + primary Užsakyti valymą (44h).
  • Active item: primary color, weight 600, 2px underline. Answers “where am I”.
  • Scroll > 8px: rgba(255,255,255,.84) + blur(14px) saturate(1.6), 1px bottom border, faint shadow. 250ms.
  • Nav links on the homepage scroll to sections. On other pages they route.

Mobile header · 64px + menu sheet

  • Logo, compact Užsakyti (44h), and menu icon button 44×44 with aria-expanded.
  • The menu is a full-screen sheet that fades and slides 8px down in 280ms. Links are 24px/600 with 62px rows and a “Čia esate” pill on the current page.
  • Language uses a segmented LT · EN · RU control, never flags. Contacts are included. The bottom has a full-width CTA plus a “Prisijungti” text link.
  • Close with the ✕ button, Esc, or by tapping a link. Focus is trapped while open and returns to the menu button on close.

Language switcher

  • Desktop: compact dropdown showing the current code (“LT”). Options show native names: Lietuvių, English, Русский.
  • Switching keeps the same page (/kainos → /en/prices) and keeps wizard data.
  • Languages are not countries, so no flags.

Each step asks one plain question as its H1. Answers are saved as the user types (“Išsaugota”). The summary and price stay visible: in a sidebar on desktop, and in the sticky bar on mobile.

Rules

  • Never reset data. State lives in a single store, persisted to localStorage as a draft (30 days). Back, forward, jumping to a step, and changing language all keep it.
  • Deep links: /uzsakymas?zingsnis=3&paslauga=generalinis&plotas=65&miestas=vilnius. The calculator uses this to skip steps it already answered.
  • Edit from review: “Keisti” jumps to that step. After it, “Tęsti” returns straight to step 7.
  • Progress: “Žingsnis 5 iš 7” + step name + a 6px bar that animates width over 400ms. The desktop stepper lets you click done steps only.
  • Focus: after a step change, focus moves to the new H1 (tabindex="-1") and the viewport scrolls to the top.
  • Slot conflicts: if a slot is taken while booking, show “Šis laikas ką tik buvo užimtas. Pasirinkite kitą.” and highlight the nearest free slot.
  • Payment: after cleaning (card, bank link, or cash). No card is needed to book, which lowers friction.

16 · Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA as the floor

  • Contrast: text 4.5:1, large text and UI boundaries 3:1. Checked in §03.
  • Focus: visible :focus-visible ring on everything, 2px, never removed. White on green surfaces.
  • Targets: 44 × 44 minimum, including toast close and carousel arrows.
  • Keyboard: select = listbox pattern (arrows, Home/End, type-ahead, Esc). Option cards are native radio groups. The before/after handle is role="slider" (arrows ±5%, Home/End). The menu sheet traps focus.
  • Live regions: price (aria-live="polite"), toasts (role="status", errors role="alert"), save indicator.
  • Forms: real <label>, aria-invalid, aria-describedby → helper + error. Autocomplete: name, tel-national, email, street-address.
  • Language: <html lang="lt">, switching updates it. Icons are aria-hidden, and icon buttons have LT labels (“Atidaryti meniu”).
  • Reduced motion: removes translate, scale, parallax, the line fill, and the shimmer, and keeps 150–200ms opacity fades.
  • Zoom: works at 200% and 400% (reflow at 320px) with no loss.
  • Low digital literacy: one question per step, plain verbs, examples in helpers, no jargon, no hidden gestures (the slider also has a visible handle and keys).
